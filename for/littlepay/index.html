The procurement windows your sales team is missing

Which states are replacing fare systems NOW?

VIC trial started March 2026. NSW trialling regionally. Perth live. Each represents a procurement decision. Your sales team needs to know which operators are in active replacement cycles.

Which operators have the oldest fare hardware?

Fleet age over 10 years correlates with fare hardware approaching end-of-life. Hardware refresh equals fare modernisation add-on. Adelaide Metro, Canberra MyWay, and Auckland AT HOP are all approaching replacement cycles.

Which operators have GTFS feeds but no open-loop?
