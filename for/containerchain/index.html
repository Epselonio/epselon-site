The Challenge

Which carriers have no digital dispatch?

Hundreds of container transport operators service Australian ports. Your sales team cannot prospect all of them. They need to know which ones are running 50+ truck fleets without real-time slot booking or container tracking.

Where does vessel bunching hit hardest?

When 3+ vessels arrive within 48 hours, carriers without digital slot booking absorb hours of truck idle time per event. The cost is invisible until you measure it — and these carriers are not measuring it.

Which operators are growing but not digitising?

Fleet size signals operational complexity. Carriers adding trucks without adding digital infrastructure are scaling their inefficiency. That is the gap Containerchain fills.

Detention and demurrage costs Australian importers and exporters hundreds of millions annually. Real-time slot booking and digital dispatch recover a fraction of that — enough to justify the platform cost from month one.

The Data Recipe

The carriers your sales team is missing

We found 10 container transport operators servicing major Australian ports without real-time slot booking or digital dispatch. Five are high-priority targets with limited digital infrastructure and significant fleet scale. Plus port volume context across Melbourne, Sydney, and Brisbane.

How we built this
Port authority volume data from Port of Melbourne (919K TEU FY24-25), Port Botany (~2.8M TEU), and Port of Brisbane (~1.4M TEU) to identify the highest-volume corridors
Fleet and operator analysis identifying container transport carriers by fleet size, service corridors, and port proximity — filtering for operators with 50+ trucks
Digital maturity audit checking each carrier for real-time tracking portals, API integrations, digital booking systems, and automated dispatch — classifying as enterprise, partial, or limited digital
Customer exclusion filtering out known Containerchain customers (Qube Holdings, ACFS Port Logistics) and carriers with existing enterprise logistics systems
