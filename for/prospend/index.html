The Challenge

The audit exposure organisations face with manual AP

Which organisations manage other people's money?

Grant-funded charities, government-regulated hospitals, franchise networks. These organisations have strict accountability requirements but often run AP on spreadsheets and email approvals.

Which ones just hit a compliance trigger?

ACNC compliance directions, APRA audit notices, government grant acquittals. These events create urgency to automate AP before the next review cycle.

Which are hiring Finance Managers?

A Finance Manager job posting signals process change. The team is growing or turning over — and the new hire will want systems, not spreadsheets.

Organisations with manual AP processes face 3.2x the audit finding rate of automated peers (CPA Australia). Average manual invoice cost: $15.20 vs $2.40 automated.
