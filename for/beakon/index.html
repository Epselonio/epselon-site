The Challenge

The compliance gap companies fill with spreadsheets until something goes wrong

Which companies just received WHS penalties?

SafeWork prosecution registers are public. But nobody is cross-referencing penalty data with SEEK job postings to identify companies actively investing in safety infrastructure right now.

Which industries still run safety on paper?

65% of construction companies still use paper-based safety systems. Agriculture, transport, and manufacturing are even further behind. These are not technology-resistant — they are under-served.

Where is regulatory pressure accelerating fastest?

Mental health claims up 161% over 10 years. WHS Regulations amended in 2025 to expand incident notification. ISO 45001 increasingly a prerequisite for government and mining tenders. The compliance bar is rising.

The median serious workers' compensation claim costs $67,400. Mental health claims average even higher. Companies managing incident reporting, contractor compliance, and audit trails on spreadsheets are one serious incident away from a penalty that dwarfs the cost of a platform like Beakon.
