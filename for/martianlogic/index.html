The Challenge

The compliance gap that keeps principals up at night

Which school groups have no integrated ATS?

Multi-campus school groups managing 500+ staff with paper-based recruitment lose candidates to schools that process applications in 24 hours. Teaching vacancies are up 23% YoY.

Are police checks and WWCC current?

Every staff member in a school requires a Working With Children Check. Every employee in healthcare needs AHPRA registration. Manual tracking across hundreds of staff is a compliance time bomb.

Do they need VEVO visa verification?

Healthcare organisations hiring international nurses need real-time visa verification. MartianLogic's VEVO integration handles this automatically. Generic HRIS platforms do not.

A single expired police check or lapsed WWCC can result in immediate suspension of operations and media scrutiny. For school groups with 500+ staff, manual compliance tracking is not a system — it is a liability.
