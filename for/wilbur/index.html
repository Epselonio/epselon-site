The Challenge

The claims gap insurers are filling with 15-year-old platforms

Which insurers have the oldest claims platforms?

APRA data and technology audit reports reveal which insurers are running claims on legacy Guidewire or custom platforms deployed 10-20 years ago. Those are the modernisation candidates.

Which MGAs are managing claims on spreadsheets?

Managing General Agents are underwriting-focused. Many handle claims on email and shared drives because they never invested in dedicated claims tech. As volume grows, the gap becomes painful.

Which boards just mandated digital transformation?

Insurer innovation teams with board-level digital transformation mandates have budget allocated but no vendor selected. LinkedIn CTO/CIO hiring signals reveal which insurers are in active evaluation.

Claims leakage costs Australian insurers an estimated 5-10% of claims spend. That is over $2.5 billion annually across the industry. Legacy platforms cannot detect or prevent it. AI-powered claims management can.
