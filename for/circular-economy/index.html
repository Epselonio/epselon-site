The Challenge

The EPR expansion nobody is systematically tracking

Which new material categories are entering EPR regulation?

Post-REDcycle soft plastics, EV batteries, e-waste. Each one will require a scheme operator with data infrastructure, collection logistics, and payments technology. Government gazettes signal these are moving from consultation to legislation.

Where will scheme operator tenders open next?

CDS Victoria launched in 2023 and is still scaling. New scheme tenders for additional material categories will follow a similar procurement process. The question is which states move first and when the RFPs drop.

Who is positioning as a competitor for new scheme operator roles?

Cleanaway, Visy, and TOMRA are all expanding recycling infrastructure. Understanding who is lobbying, tendering, and partnering for new EPR schemes tells you where the competitive pressure will come from.

Australia's container deposit schemes alone recover over 3 billion containers per year. New EPR material categories (e-waste, soft plastics, batteries) represent a comparable volume of infrastructure build. The scheme operators appointed in the next 24 months will lock in multi-year contracts.
