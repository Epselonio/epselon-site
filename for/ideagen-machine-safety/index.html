The Challenge

The compliance gap organisations are filling with clipboards and spreadsheets

Which councils have no digital plant compliance?

There are 537 local councils across Australia. Plant Assessor serves 150. That leaves 387 councils operating mowers, trucks, excavators, and compactors without systematic risk assessment software.

Which contractors are scaling without plant pre-qualification?

Principal contractors now mandate plant pre-qualification from subcontractors. Tier 2 construction and mining services companies winning new projects need to demonstrate compliance — and most are still doing it on paper.

Where is regulatory enforcement tightening fastest?

SafeWork NSW ran targeted blitzes on unsafe mobile plant and machinery. Safe Work Australia reported 188 fatalities in 2024. Regulators are moving from education to enforcement. Organisations without documented compliance are exposed.

The average WHS prosecution in Australia results in fines of $200,000-$500,000+ per incident. For organisations without documented risk assessments and pre-start checks, every piece of heavy machinery is an unmitigated liability.
