The Challenge

The FX spread gap SME importers are paying without knowing it

Which corridors have the widest FX spreads?

Major banks charge SMEs 1.5-2.5% above mid-market on FX transactions versus 0.2-0.5% for institutional clients. On a $10M import book, that is $150,000-250,000 in hidden cost per year.

Which industries import the most?

Manufacturing, textiles, IT services, automotive parts, food. ABS data shows exactly which corridors have the highest volume — and which ones are growing fastest (India +22%, Vietnam +14%).

Where is trade finance underserved?

68% of Australian SME importers report difficulty accessing trade finance from major banks. $12 billion in unmet demand. Your CAFIN trade finance arm addresses exactly this gap.
