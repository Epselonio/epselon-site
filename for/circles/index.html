Which operators are evaluating BSS alternatives?

Tier-1 operators do not publicise BSS evaluations. The signals are buried in procurement tenders, CTO keynotes, and vendor partnership announcements. Your BD team needs to know which ones are active.

Where are Amdocs and Netcracker contracts expiring?

Legacy BSS contracts run 5-7 years. The ones signed in 2019-2020 are coming up for renewal. Those operators are your highest-value prospects — they already know they need to modernise.

Which markets have the weakest incumbent lock-in?

Some operators are 20 years deep with Amdocs. Others are greenfield digital brands with no legacy. The opportunity set looks completely different depending on the market.
