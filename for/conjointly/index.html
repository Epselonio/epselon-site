The Challenge

The companies guessing their pricing to market

Which companies are launching without research?

IP Australia processes thousands of trademark filings every quarter. Companies filing in Class 5 (pharma), Class 29-33 (food and beverage), and Class 3 (cosmetics) are signalling upcoming product launches. Most have no dedicated research function.

When is the research window?

Trademark filing to product launch is typically 6-12 months. That is the window where conjoint analysis, Van Westendorp pricing, and claims testing have the highest impact. After launch, it is too late. Before filing, there is nothing to test.

Why can't they afford traditional research?

Qualtrics starts at $13K+. Sawtooth runs $4.5-12K annually. Most mid-market FMCG, pharma, and SaaS companies cannot justify enterprise research pricing for a single product launch. Conjointly starts at $158/month.

Research shows that products launched without pricing research fail at 2-3x the rate of those with validated pricing. For a mid-market FMCG launch, that is the difference between a $500K product that finds its market and one that gets pulled from shelves in 6 months.
