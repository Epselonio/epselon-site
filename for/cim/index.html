The Challenge

The building performance gap REITs are solving with consultants and spreadsheets

Which buildings are most exposed?

The NABERS gas change affects every office building with gas-fired HVAC. Most REITs do not know which assets in their portfolio will lose a star until they run the numbers. That analysis is happening now or not at all.

What does each star actually cost?

Research shows each NABERS star improvement is worth 2-3% in valuation premium. For a $500M portfolio, a one-star drop across 10 buildings is tens of millions in eroded value. But most REITs cannot quantify that exposure today.

How do they report it to investors?

ASRS mandatory climate disclosure is now live for Group 1 entities. GRESB scores directly impact investor allocation. REITs need continuous building analytics, not annual audits. The reporting cycle has compressed.

Up to 70% of currently rated office buildings could see their NABERS star rating fall under the updated gas consumption treatment. For REITs with 2-3% valuation premium per star, that is a material balance sheet risk.
