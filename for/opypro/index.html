The Challenge

The AR gap wholesalers are filling with manual follow-ups

Which wholesalers have the worst debtor days?

The average AU wholesaler waits 47 days for payment. The worst quartile waits 72+ days. Those are the businesses where AR automation delivers immediate, measurable ROI.

Which verticals have the most complex invoicing?

Building materials with project-based billing. F&B with hundreds of retail accounts. Agricultural with seasonal cycles. Each vertical has different AR pain that manual processes cannot solve at scale.

Which distributors just lost revenue to bad debt?

ASIC filings show bad debt write-offs in annual reports. The wholesaler that just wrote off $200K in uncollectable receivables is the warmest prospect for AR automation you will ever find.

Mid-market wholesalers lose 2-4% of revenue annually to AR inefficiency. Wrong allocations, missed follow-ups, and write-offs. For a $20M wholesaler, that is $400K-800K per year in preventable loss.
