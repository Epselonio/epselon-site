The Challenge

The clinical workflow gap pharmacies are filling with paper

Which pharmacies are prescribing-ready?

Pharmacist prescribing is live in QLD and expanding to every state. But most pharmacies have no digital workflow for clinical assessment documentation, prescription records, or follow-up scheduling. They are using paper forms and manual PBS claims.

Which states are expanding scope next?

Regulatory timelines are staggered and complex. QLD went permanent July 2025. NSW launches 2026. VIC, SA, and ACT are expanding. Each state has different conditions, training requirements, and rollout dates. Nobody is mapping this in one place.

Which pharmacy groups will adopt first?

National chains like Chemist Warehouse (3,800+ pharmacies) make group-level technology decisions. Regional chains in QLD where prescribing is already live are training staff now. The adoption curve is predictable but nobody is tracking it against digital readiness.

Pharmacist prescribing generates $35-80 per consultation compared to $8-15 for dispensing. A pharmacy doing 10 prescribing consultations per day adds $350-800 in daily revenue. That revenue needs clinical workflow software to capture, document, and claim.
