Why WideSky

Three things caught our attention.

First, the Project Haystack standards alignment means WideSky speaks the same data language as modern BMS systems. That is a technical moat that generic IoT platforms cannot match for building and energy applications.

Second, Barangaroo South is the highest-profile smart precinct in Australia. Having that as a reference customer opens doors with every premium commercial property portfolio in the country.

Third, NABERS compliance is moving from annual to continuous reporting. Buildings that rely on quarterly meter reads are operating blind between measurement periods — and that is exactly the gap a real-time IoT data platform fills.

That pattern means one thing: the buildings running disparate BMS systems without a unified data layer are the ones most exposed to compliance gaps and energy cost overruns. WideSky needs to be in front of them before they attempt to build it themselves.
