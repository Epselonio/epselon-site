The Challenge

The readiness gap your prospects are filling with spreadsheets

Which nations are in the procurement gap?

18 nations are operating or procuring F-35 alone. Add Gripen, Typhoon, and Rafale and the number grows further. Your BD team cannot track every defence procurement announcement across every nation. They need to know which ones just committed to a platform but have not awarded the training system contract.

Which OEM channels create warm introductions?

Aircraft manufacturers like Lockheed Martin, Saab, and Airbus maintain relationships with procuring nations. Mapping which OEM channel applies to each nation creates a structured path to the decision-maker rather than cold outreach to a defence ministry.

What happens when the training gap is not filled?

Nations that procure aircraft without an integrated training management system build internal workarounds. Spreadsheets, email chains, disconnected briefing systems. Once embedded, those workarounds are extremely difficult to displace. The window to win the contract is before that happens.

Nations that delay training system procurement by even 12 months after aircraft delivery typically face 6-12 months of reduced operational readiness as pilots and ground crews train on improvised systems. That is the cost of the gap FlightPRO closes.
