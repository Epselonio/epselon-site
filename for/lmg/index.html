The Challenge

The broker recruitment gap every aggregator is ignoring

Which brokers are in evaluation mode?

There are 18,000 active mortgage brokers in Australia. Your recruitment team cannot chase all of them. They need to know which high-volume brokers at competitor networks are showing switching signals right now.

Which aggregators are losing brokers?

Bank-owned aggregators face an independence question. Post-acquisition integrations create uncertainty. These are not static lists. Broker sentiment shifts quarterly and the ASIC register shows movement in near real-time.

Where are the geographic gaps?

Regional brokers outside capital cities are consistently underserved by metro-focused aggregators. These brokers settle significant volume but rarely get the same attention as CBD offices. That is a recruitment opportunity.

A single high-volume broker settling $50M+ annually represents $200K+ in trail revenue over the lifetime of the relationship. Winning 10 of these brokers from competitor networks pays for an entire year of systematic recruitment infrastructure.
