The Challenge

The CX gap hiding inside annual reports

Who reports NPS but does not act on it?

40 ASX companies publish NPS in their annual reports. Most collect it quarterly. By the time the number reaches the board, the customers who scored low have already churned.

Why quarterly surveys are a lagging indicator

A quarterly NPS survey tells you what happened 90 days ago. Real-time closed-loop feedback tells you what is happening now. The difference is whether you retain the customer or read about losing them in next quarter's report.

The enterprise CX vendor trap

Qualtrics and Medallia take months to deploy, cost six figures, and require dedicated teams. Companies that signed those contracts 3 years ago are approaching renewal with buyer's remorse. Resonate deploys in 2 weeks.

Companies relying on quarterly NPS surveys face a 60-90 day blind spot between when a customer experience fails and when leadership knows about it. That blind spot is where churn happens.
