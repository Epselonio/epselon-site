The Challenge

The reputation gap hiding in plain sight

Which brands have the biggest location gap?

Multi-location brands with 50+ sites cannot manually audit review performance. The gap between their best and worst locations is invisible to head office until it costs them customers.

Which locations are losing local pack rankings?

Locations with fewer than 50 reviews and response times over 24 hours rank significantly lower in Google local pack. That is foot traffic walking to a competitor.

Which industries are most exposed?

Dental, fitness, automotive, and real estate franchises depend on local search. A single unresponded negative review at one location drags down the entire brand perception.

Locations with active review management and sub-24hr response times rank 3.2x higher in Google local pack than locations with similar ratings but slower response. Most multi-location brands have a 40%+ gap between their best and worst performers.
