The Challenge

The contact data your prospects lose at every conference

Which companies are scaling field sales fastest?

There are hundreds of growing AU tech companies. Blinq's sales team cannot chase all of them. They need to know which ones are hiring field reps, sponsoring conferences, and still exchanging paper cards at the booth.

Where are contacts falling through the cracks?

A field rep meets 30-50 people at a conference. They scan a badge, grab a card, type a name into their phone. Three weeks later, 60% of those contacts have not made it into the CRM. The conversation is lost.

Which companies lack standardised contact capture?

Companies with 100+ sales reps using a mix of personal phone apps, paper cards, and badge scanners have no way to enforce brand consistency or CRM data quality. That is a system problem Blinq solves.

A field sales team of 20 attending 4 conferences a year generates 2,400-4,000 new contacts annually. Without standardised capture, 60%+ never reach the CRM. That is 1,400-2,400 lost conversations per year — per company.
