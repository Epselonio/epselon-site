Which venues are most likely to default?

Hospitality insolvencies are at record levels. Your suppliers are extending credit to venues that may not survive the quarter. Order frequency and payment pattern data could predict this — but it is not being surfaced.

Which suppliers carry the most exposure?

A supplier with 200 venue accounts has counterparty risk on every order. If 5% of those venues go insolvent, that is uncollectable revenue. The payment guarantee layer reduces that exposure — but suppliers need to quantify it first.
