The Challenge

The onboarding gap costing employers millions

Which employers have the highest onboarding volume?

Hospitality, retail, and aged care employers with 40%+ turnover are cycling thousands of new starters through manual processes every year. Each one is a compliance event, a benefits enrollment, and a retention decision.

Where is Fair Work exposure greatest?

Fair Work penalties now include criminal offences for intentional underpayment. Manual onboarding with complex award structures is the number one source of payroll errors. Every new starter onboarded without digital capture is risk on the balance sheet.

Which employers are losing candidates to better benefits?

The EV FBT exemption changed the game. Employers offering novated leasing and salary packaging attract better talent. The ones that do not are losing candidates to competitors who do — especially in sectors where base salaries are compressed.

Australian employee turnover costs $3.8 billion annually. Digital onboarding reduces underpayment risk by 73% and first-90-day attrition by 28%. Every onboarding cycle Flare captures is revenue retained.
