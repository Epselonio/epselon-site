The Challenge

Delivery friction is a measurable revenue problem

Which retailers have the highest delivery complaint rates?

Review sentiment on Trustpilot and ProductReview reveals which retailers have the worst delivery experience. Missed deliveries, stolen parcels, return complexity — all quantifiable.

Which ecommerce stores have no PUDO option at checkout?

35,500+ Australian Shopify stores in the CartInsight database. Most have no out-of-home collection option. That is a conversion and returns problem waiting to be solved.

Which retailers are about to hit Scope 3 reporting?

From July 2026, companies over $200M revenue must report Scope 3 emissions — including last-mile delivery. PUDO cuts those emissions by approximately 50%. That makes HUBBED a compliance solution.

Australian online spending hit $82.6 billion in 2025 (+14% YoY). Parcel locker deliveries grew 18%. But two in three shoppers will not return to a retailer after a poor delivery experience. Failed deliveries are not just a logistics cost — they are a customer retention crisis.

Review sentiment analysis — scraped Trustpilot and ProductReview for delivery-related complaints at major AU retailers
