The Challenge

High-volume airlines still running cargo on fax machines and emails

Which airlines have the widest digital gap?

Some airlines move millions in cargo revenue but still process bookings manually. Your platform already serves 104+ airlines. The question is which of the remaining carriers have the volume to justify digitisation and the urgency to move fast.

Which regions are digitising slowest?

Regional APAC carriers and select Middle Eastern airlines are behind global carriers on digital booking adoption. These are the markets where the ONE Record mandate creates the most pressure and the biggest opportunity window.

What happens when ONE Record hits?

IATA ONE Record mandates standardised digital data sharing across the air cargo supply chain. Airlines that are not digitally ready face compliance costs, forwarder defection to digital-first carriers, and route-level revenue at risk.

Airlines that digitise cargo booking processes see 15-25% improvement in load factor through better pricing, faster forwarder confirmation, and reduced no-shows. Manual airlines are leaving revenue on the tarmac.
