Why SwitchDin

Three things caught our attention.

First, APAC Distributed Energy Asset Optimisation Company of the Year 2026. That is not a participation award — it means the platform is validated at scale with real utility customers (Ausgrid, AGL, Origin, Horizon Power).

Second, AEMO's Integrated System Plan targets 100% renewables by 2050. The grid cannot get there without DER orchestration. Every megawatt of rooftop solar, every battery, every EV charger needs to be visible and controllable. That is SwitchDin's entire value proposition.

Third, dynamic export limits are now live across multiple DNSPs. The old model of fixed export limits is dead. Utilities need real-time DER management — and most are still operating with legacy SCADA systems that cannot see behind the meter.

That pattern means one thing: every DNSP and retailer in Australia needs a DER management platform. SwitchDin has the product and the reference customers — the question is how fast you can systematically reach the rest.
