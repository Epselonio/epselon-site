The documentation wall your prospects are hitting

Which practices just added clinicians but wait times did not improve?

The November bulk billing expansion pushed volume into practices that hired more GPs. But documentation overhead scales with headcount. More clinicians means more notes, more referrals, more admin.

Which regions saw the biggest billing rate rebound?

SA3 regions where bulk billing rates dropped 10-18% between 2022-2024 then rebounded post-policy change. These are the areas where volume surged fastest.

Which practices are NOT on an AI scribe yet?

You have 50% of GPs. Lyrebird has Best Practice backing. The remaining 50% need to be reached at the moment of maximum pain. That moment is now.

Heidi reduces documentation time per consult by 51%. For a practice adding 3 clinicians, that is hundreds of hours per year recovered. The documentation wall is not a technology problem. It is a timing problem.
