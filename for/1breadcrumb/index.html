The Challenge

The compliance gap your prospects are filling with clipboards

Which builders are scaling fastest?

There are 431,000 construction businesses in Australia. Your sales team cannot chase all of them. They need to know which Tier 2 head contractors are growing project volumes faster than their compliance systems can handle.

Which builders just lost (or will lose) a tender on compliance?

Government infrastructure tenders increasingly mandate digital WHS. Builders still on paper SWMS are disqualified before they finish the submission. That data is in state procurement records nobody is cross-referencing against your CRM.

Which states are tightening WHS enforcement?

NSW WHS Regulations changed August 2025. QLD is scaling enforcement ahead of the Olympics. VBA compliance requirements are tightening. Builders in these states who are still on paper have a deadline they may not know about.

A single SafeWork prohibition notice costs a builder an average of $50,000-$250,000 in project delays, legal fees, and lost contracts. Digital safety systems reduce WHS incidents by up to 40%. That is revenue your platform protects.
