The Challenge

The questions every surcharging merchant will ask in Q3

Do I absorb 1.5-2.8% on every debit transaction?

On a $500 furniture order, that is $7.50-$14 per sale the merchant currently passes to the customer. From July, they eat it. On 200 orders a month, that is $1,500-$2,800 in new costs.

Is there a payment rail with zero chargebacks?

Card-based payments carry 1-2% chargeback rates in e-commerce. PayTo bank payments are irrevocable. The funds move directly between bank accounts. No disputes. No reversals.

Can I get settlement faster than T+2 days?

Card settlement takes 1-3 business days. PayTo settles in real time. For a Shopify merchant doing $50k a month, that is $50k freed from payment float every single month.

A high-AOV Shopify merchant processing $100k/month in debit card transactions at a 2% surcharge currently passes $2,000/month to customers. From July 2026, that $2,000 comes off their margin — unless they switch payment rails.
