Which companies process 10K+ invoices manually?

ASX mid-cap companies with 900+ branches or 1000+ locations generate massive invoice volume. Manual processing at this scale means duplicate payments, data entry errors, and missed early payment discounts.

Are they Peppol-ready?

The Australian Government's Peppol eInvoicing mandate is accelerating. Companies without digital invoice processing will be forced to adopt. Early movers get efficiency gains now.

Do they run SAP?

SpendConsole's SAP integration is a differentiator. Companies on SAP looking for AP automation have fewer options than those on Xero or MYOB. That is SpendConsole's sweet spot.
