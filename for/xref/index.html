Which employers hire thousands per year?

Government departments, hospital groups, and universities process thousands of new hires annually. Each hire requires 2-3 reference checks. That is tens of thousands of manual phone calls and emails per year.

Which ones still do it manually?

Manual reference checking averages 23 days. Automated checking via Xref averages 24 hours. For employers competing for talent, 22 days of delay means losing candidates to faster-moving competitors.

Do they need background screening too?

Government and healthcare employers require police checks, WWCC, and credential verification on top of references. Xref's Trust Marketplace bundles all three. One vendor, one integration.

An employer hiring 5,000 people per year with 2 references each spends 10,000+ hours annually on manual reference checking. At $50/hour loaded cost, that is $500K+ in HR labour. Xref automates 90% of it.
