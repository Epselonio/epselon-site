The Challenge

The AP gap that costs builders millions every year

Which builders are managing invoices in spreadsheets?

Commercial builders running 30+ active projects process thousands of subcontractor invoices monthly. Without project-level AP visibility, cost overruns stay hidden until it is too late.

Which ones use Xero or MYOB?

Lentune integrates with both. Builders already on Xero or MYOB are pre-qualified for a frictionless implementation. The accounting stack is the qualifying signal.

Are they growing fast enough to feel the pain?

Builders under $500M in revenue can often manage manually. Above $700M, the volume of subcontractor invoices and project variations makes spreadsheets dangerous.

Commercial builders processing subcontractor invoices manually typically see 3-5% in payment errors and disputes per project. On a $100M project, that is $3-5M in cash flow friction.
