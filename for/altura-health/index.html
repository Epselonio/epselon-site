The Challenge

The compliance gap your prospects are filling with paper and legacy systems

Which day hospitals are due for re-accreditation?

NSQHS accreditation runs on a 3-year cycle. Every cycle is a procurement trigger. Day hospitals on legacy PAS face the choice: pass the assessment on paper, or upgrade to a cloud system that generates compliance data automatically.

Which hospitals are still running desktop PAS?

SimDay and other legacy systems were built for a world where assessors gave weeks of notice. Since July 2023, they give 24 hours. Desktop PAS cannot produce real-time clinical data across sites. That is the gap FYDO fills.

Which specialties are growing fastest?

IVF, cosmetic, orthopaedic, ophthalmology, gastroenterology. Each specialty has different billing complexity and compliance needs. The fastest-growing segments need cloud PAS most urgently — and have the budget to buy it.

A conditional accreditation outcome from an NSQHS assessment can directly impact private health insurer contracts and referral pipeline. Day hospitals on legacy PAS are carrying compliance risk they may not be quantifying.
