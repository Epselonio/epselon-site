The Data Recipe

The associations your sales team is missing

How we built this
Event listings and conference directories identifying major Australian professional associations running annual conferences with large sponsor programs (50+ exhibitors or sponsors)
Sponsor prospectus and post-event reports reviewed for each association to identify what metrics they report to sponsors (total attendance vs session-level engagement vs lead capture)
Platform detection checked each association's registration and event pages for visible event management technology (Cvent, Eventbrite, Bizzabo, or custom-built)
Sponsor rebooking analysis cross-referenced industry data on sponsor retention rates against engagement reporting capabilities to quantify the gap
