The Challenge

The compliance gap most MSPs cannot close

Which agencies need IRAP-assessed providers?

Every non-corporate Commonwealth entity must reach Essential Eight Maturity Level 2 across all 8 controls. Their MSPs need to meet the same bar. Most do not.

Which contracts are approaching renewal?

AusTender publishes contract values and expiry dates. State procurement portals do the same. The data is public. The question is who is cross-referencing it against compliance deadlines.

Which current MSPs lack the compliance credentials?

Many agencies use MSPs that were appointed before IRAP requirements tightened. When the contract renews, the compliance bar is higher. That is the window.

PSPF Section 14.2 requires all non-corporate Commonwealth entities to implement Essential Eight Maturity Level 2 across every control. Agencies that cannot demonstrate compliance face audit findings and remediation orders. Their MSP is the first place the auditor looks.
