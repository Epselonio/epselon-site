The Challenge

The governance gap Copilot just made urgent

Which organisations just approved Copilot pilots?

70% of Fortune 500 have adopted Copilot. Australian enterprises are following. But most are stuck at pilot stage because Copilot will surface whatever data it can see. If SharePoint is ungoverned, Copilot is ungoverned.

Which industries face the harshest regulatory exposure?

APRA CPS 234 for banks. PSPF Section 14 for government. Privacy Act for health. AML/CTF Tranche 2 for lawyers from July 2026. Each regulation makes ungoverned AI deployment a compliance breach waiting to happen.

Who is trying to classify data manually?

Large organisations with millions of documents across SharePoint, file shares, and legacy systems. Manual classification projects take years and cost millions. EncompaaS does it with AI in weeks. The organisations attempting manual approaches are your most urgent prospects.
