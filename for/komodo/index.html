The Challenge

The measurement gap schools are filling with annual surveys and gut feel

Which schools are marketing wellbeing without measuring it?

Hundreds of schools put "wellbeing" on their homepage and prospectus. Very few can show parents cohort-level data, intervention tracking, or trend analysis. That gap is about to become a liability.

Which regions just introduced wellbeing mandates?

Victoria's 2026 mandate is the headline. But NSW, QLD, NZ, and international accreditation bodies are all tightening requirements. The regulatory wave is rolling across markets one by one.

Which international schools charge $50K tuition but cannot evidence wellbeing outcomes?

Premium international schools compete on pastoral care. Parents choosing between two $50K-per-year schools will pick the one that can show them wellbeing data. The school without evidence loses enrolments.

Schools that advertise wellbeing programs without systematic measurement face reputational and regulatory risk as mandates take effect. The gap between marketing promises and measurable outcomes is where Komodo fits.
