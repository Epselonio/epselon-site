How we built this
ABN Bulk Extract (data.gov.au) identifying professional services firms registered 2-5 years ago, still active, filtered by ANZSIC codes for Professional/Scientific/Technical Services
SEEK and Indeed job postings cross-referenced against those firms to find agencies hiring their 3rd-5th employee, especially project coordinators or account managers
G2 and Capterra competitor reviews monitoring negative reviews on Harvest, Monday.com, and Toggl from professional services firms complaining about missing invoicing, time tracking, or resource scheduling
