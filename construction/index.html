The gap

The work is announced in public. Your reps hear about it last.

The tools software companies use to spot likely buyers were never built for your industry. None of them read tender portals, planning registers or ASX announcements. So in the construction supply chain, reps still run on a stale account list and whatever they happen to hear on site.

Who just won work in your patch?

A contractor that wins a big job starts buying within weeks: plant, materials, subbies, services. The award is published while the early packages are still open. Most suppliers find out by word of mouth, when the order has already gone to someone else.

Which accounts are worth a call this week?

Your reps' list was right in January. Since then projects have finished, contacts have moved on and new players have won work. Nobody has the time to rebuild it, so the same names keep getting the same call.

Why are you always the second call?

By the time a won job travels by word of mouth, the early packages are locked in. The supplier who calls first sells on timing. Everyone who calls after them quotes on price.

The signal is already sitting in public records. It just never reaches your reps in time. That's the gap Epselon Signals closes.
