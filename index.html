The gap

Every sales-intelligence tool was built for tech. Your industry got nothing.

ZoomInfo, Bombora, Cognism, Apollo. The whole category grew up serving software companies, because software buyers were the easiest to track. If you supply builders, distribute industrial gear, move freight or broker commercial cover, none of those tools watch the places your buying signals actually appear.

The tools skip your market

Type "tender win" or "new depot" into a SaaS intent tool and you get nothing. Those signals live in tender portals, planning registers and trade press the tools never index.

Reps run on a stale list

The account list was right eighteen months ago. Since then projects have finished, contacts have moved on and new players have won work. Nobody has time to rebuild it, so the same names keep getting the same call.

You hear about the deal too late

By the time a won job travels by word of mouth, the early packages are locked in. The supplier who calls first sells on timing. Everyone after them quotes on price.

The signal already exists in public. It just never reaches your reps in time. That's the gap we close.

How it works

From a public record to your rep's task list.

1

We read the record

Every week we sweep tender portals, planning registers, ASX announcements, job boards and trade press across your territory. On top of that standard watch, we find and prove sources specific to your patch. Those are usually where the best signals hide.

2

We verify every signal

Each one carries a named company, a date and a link to the record it came from, so your reps can check it themselves before picking up the phone. When a signal can't be verified, we drop it. You get eight real signals before you ever get ten padded ones.

3

It reaches your reps where they work

The app is the home base. The push into your CRM or inbox is what makes it turn into calls.

FAQ

Straight answers.

Where do the signals come from?

Public and licensed sources. Tender portals, council and state planning registers, ASX announcements, job boards and trade press, plus sources specific to your market that we find and prove during the build. Every signal links to where it came from.

How do I know the signals are real?

Each one carries a named company, a date and a link to the public record. Check any of them yourself. When something can't be verified, we drop it rather than pad your feed.

What does the demo show?

The full app, loaded with example data for a fictional supplier. What you would buy is the same app pointed at your market, in your brand, behind your login.

We already have an SDR team or an agency doing outbound.

Good. That's the ideal starting point, and we're not replacing them. Most outbound teams work lists built on firmographics. We add the timing layer that tells them which accounts to work this week. Your team still does the selling.

Is our data safe?

Your feed is yours alone. Every client runs on a dedicated database, logins are restricted to your company's email domains, and nothing about your market watch is shared. We ship an ISO 27001 control mapping with every build. That is a mapping against the standard, not a certification, and we'll walk you through exactly what it covers.

What does it cost?

Builds are fixed-scope and priced on a call, because the price depends on your territory, the sources involved and how many teams use it. The way in is cheap: we show you your own market first, then you decide.

What happens on the call?

Twenty minutes. You tell us what you sell, who buys it and the patch you cover. We tell you which signals are actually trackable for your market. Sometimes the honest answer is "few", and we'll say so.
